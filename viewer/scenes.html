molla — Rust + WGPU Physics Engine

Scene catalog

The molla_viewer ships with 101 self-contained scenes spanning every solver, kernel, sensor, and FEM stepper the engine implements. Pick one from the dropdown in the top-left of the viewer, or jump to a marquee scene via a hotkey.

This chapter is the index. The scenes are grouped by topic; for the strictly numerical ordering see Scene::label() in bin/molla_viewer/src/main.rs.

Catalog summary

  • 101 scenes total, numbered #01–#101 in the dropdown.
  • 12 marquee scenes have single-letter hotkeys.
  • ~30 are direct ports of Warp examples (prefix Warp*).
  • ~25 are Newton-port scenes covering the Newton example tree.
  • ~12 are FEM steppers (Stages 36–46).
  • The remainder are original molla demos built around specific Stage deliverables.

Each scene’s source lives in bin/molla_viewer/src/main.rs. Search for the variant name (e.g. Scene::ImplicitMpmBingham) to find the build_scene arm, the per-scene state struct, the update system (if any), and the control_pane arm.

See viewer/overview.md for the architecture behind the catalog, and roadmap/stages.md for the chronological stage history that produced each scene.
