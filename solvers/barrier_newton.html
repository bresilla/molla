molla — Rust + WGPU Physics Engine

Barrier-Newton contact (IPC-family)

The penetration-free contact backend (roadmap C0): interpenetration is mathematically impossible at every Newton iterate, not just at convergence. Designed after the comparison with ZOZO’s ppf-contact-solver; molla targets the same invariant at viewer scale (10k–50k vertices) rather than the 180M-contact offline regime.

Formulation

Each step minimizes the incremental potential

E(x) = ½‖x − x̃‖²_M + h²·[ elastic(x) + Σ barrier(d_c(x)) + strain(x) ] + friction(x)
x̃    = x_prev + h·v + h²·g

with the cubic compact-support barrier b(d) = κ(d̂−d)³/(3d̂²) — C² at d̂, convex, zero outside the activation band (no log singularity, no global κ tuning). κ is per-contact and elasticity-inclusive by default: κ = scale·(m + h²·Σk_incident)/h², the TOG 43.6 scheme — a stiff material meets a proportionally stiff barrier (the 1 kN elastic-press oracle holds 11.4× more band margin than mass-only κ). Damped Newton with matrix-free PCG (block mass-diagonal preconditioner, warm-started from the previous direction with blocked early exit on the GPU), and the piece that carries the guarantee: a CCD-truncated line search — step length capped at 0.9×TOI before Armijo backtracking, so no trial state ever crosses a surface.

Every Hessian term follows one projection discipline (SPD only): compression-clamped springs, convex-part barriers, Gauss-part tet volumes, positive-slope friction.

What it covers

  • Obstacles: analytic half-space + sphere contacts.
  • Self-collision: non-adjacent vertex-triangle and edge-edge pairs with clamped closest-point weights (exact-a.e. gradients; the measure-zero kinks at aligned-grid degeneracies are documented). Candidates at 3·d̂ plus a global travel cap α·max|p| ≤ d̂ make the additive-CCD argument airtight: an unseen pair cannot reach contact.
  • Volumetric tets: ½k_v(V−V₀)² with exact volume gradients.
  • Hard strain limiting: the bound comes from line-search truncation (a limit crossing is a TOI); the strain barrier (κ_s = scale·k·rest², elasticity-derived) only keeps springs off the wall. An unlimited 15.8× stretch holds at 1.0486 < 1.05.
  • Lagged IPC friction with a true μ_s/μ_k split: normal force and frame frozen at step start; C¹ mollifier (stick well to μ_s·λ, blend, kinetic plateau μ_k·λ). The discriminating incline (tan θ between μ_k and μ_s) sticks at 0.83 mm creep vs 0.44 m slide — the oracle velocity-level friction cannot pass in principle. Pair (cloth-on- cloth) friction uses the same recipe on the relative stencil slip.
  • True pinning: DoF removal (x̃ = x, zero gradient row, identity Hessian row). A huge mass does NOT pin under an incremental potential — the predictor free-falls independently of mass.
  • Rigid coupling, two ways: WEAK (BarrierSolver) — obstacles follow body poses, third-law-exact reaction wrenches, and a rigid-side TOI that closes the tunneling hole; and STRONG (ABD) — bodies as 12-DOF affine transforms INSIDE the Newton solve via proxy vertices (add_affine_body), one line search protecting everything, momentum machine-exact (5e-15) with no exchange approximation.

API

CPU f64 oracle: molla_solvers::barrier_contact::BarrierShell (new → with_triangles / with_tets / pin_vertices, step, invariants min_gap / max_strain). GPU f32: molla_kernels::BarrierNewtonGpu (new / new_with_mesh / new_full), host-driven Newton over device gradient/Hessian/PCG/TOI with 4-byte scalar readbacks and the f32 convergence freeze.

Pair discovery runs on device through Teschner-hash uniform grids (cell ≥ primitive extent + 3d̂ ⇒ single-ring probes are complete; hash collisions only add candidates; bucket overflow is a hard error), or — for adversarial primitive-size distributions — through a step-granularity LBVH broad phase (use_lbvh): AABBs inflated by the travel-cap bound keep the candidate set a superset for the whole step, and per-iterate filter kernels do the exact tests.

Pair (cloth-on-cloth) friction runs on BOTH backends: the device freezes the pair set, frames, λ and anchors at step start (pair_friction_lag) — the layer-on-layer discriminator lands the same 0.62 mm hold on GPU as the f64 oracle.

The PPF example gallery (viewer scenes 126–143)

Every example from st-tech/ppf-contact-solver has a molla rendition on this backend: drape/curtain (126), the twist family — twist, five-twist and both large variants — as a pre-twisted pinned strip (127), friction ramp with live μ sliders (128), strain-limited hang (129), sheet stack (130), trampoline via weak rigid coupling (131), trapped (132), needle (133, sphere tip), belt/roller over sphere pulleys (134), noodle/yarn strand piles (135), a woven lattice held together purely by EE contact (136), a tightening fishing knot (137), domino chains and card A-frames as ABD boxes (138/139), mixed codimension — sheet + strands + rigid box in ONE solve (140), garment fitting on a sphere mannequin (141), and a buckling ribbon (142). Boundary DRIVERS from the original notebooks (twisting ends, motorized rollers) are replaced by equivalent initial conditions or static stand-ins, noted per scene; plastic.ipynb (yield) is intentionally unsupported and walkthrough.ipynb maps to this chapter. Their fail-examples/ gallery is mirrored FAITHFULLY (barrier_fail_examples.rs): the solver REFUSES intersecting starts — distance scan plus a Möller edge–triangle PIERCING scan, since two crossed sheets report a positive closest distance — and refuses nonsense parameters (BarrierParams::validate), with an explicit opt-out (reject_intersecting_start = false) whose recovery mode lets the barrier push out of violation (CCD skips already-violated contacts). Their blender/pdrd_chain.py becomes scene 143 (rigid log-pile — interlocking ring links await torus geometry). A headless smoke test drives every scene’s REAL build code for 30 steps and asserts the invariant.

Two rod-modelling facts the gallery forced into the solver: explicit collision edges carry chain-run provenance and near-neighbours (≤ 3 hops) are excluded from EE pairing — and an EXACTLY straight chain is degenerate anyway (all segments mutually colinear at distance zero), so strand scenes carry natural curvature, as real yarn does.

Parity philosophy

f32-vs-f64 drapes are non-unique equilibria: the GPU drop test gates on ENERGY (the f32 drape found a 13% deeper minimum than the oracle), plus the invariant, the resting-gap band, and — for self-collision — a host-side exact intersection scan on read-back positions. Every test in barrier_*.rs ends with an explicit intersection scan.
